# Species archetypes

The parameters that make each archetype read. Adjust them to the reference.

| Archetype | Habit | Branching | Foliage | Tells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Broadleaf, round (oak, maple, lime) | Dome crown, wider with age | 3–4 levels, wide angles, sinuous limbs | Leaf cards 6–15 cm, clustered at the twig tips | Visible scaffold limbs. Gaps in the crown show sky. |
| Broadleaf, upright (poplar, columnar) | Narrow and tall | Steep, short children | Small dense leaves | Strong vertical rhythm |
| Flowering tree (cherry) | Spreading, layered | Horizontal tiers, dark bark with lenticels | Flowers in clusters at the nodes: 5-petal blossoms with receptacles, buds, a few leaves | Blossom masses with real flowers, never pink balls |
| Conifer, spruce/fir | Conical | Whorls of near-horizontal branches every 0.3–0.6 m, drooping tips | Needle sprays as cards along the branchlets | Stiff in wind. Layered silhouette. |
| Pine (umbrella/Scots) | A tall clear trunk, a flat or rounded crown | Few heavy limbs | Needle tufts at the branch tips | Orange-brown upper bark |
| Cypress (Italian) | A narrow column | Many short upright branches | Dense scale foliage | Column silhouette with a pointed top |
| Palm | A single ringed trunk, a slight curve | None. A crown of 15–30 fronds. | Each frond a rachis with leaflets, older fronds drooping | Dead frond bases under the crown |
| Shrub | Multi-stem, from a root crown | Many thin stems | Small leaves along the stems | No single trunk. Base in the soil. |
| Topiary (only where the reference shows it) | Clipped shape | A real dense twig structure under the shell | Many small leaves forming the clipped surface | Still has a trunk and stems inside, visible at the base |
| Vine / ivy | Follows a surface | Stems along the surface, with adhesive roots | Leaves on petioles from the stem, overlapping | Stems visible between leaves. Grows from the ground or a pot. |
| Grass clump | Tufts | Blades from a crown | — | Blades bend and cross. Colour varies from base to tip. |
| Crop (wheat, barley) | Rows of stalks | A stalk, leaves, a head | — | Rows read at distance; heads catch the light |
