# Interpretation write-up

Write this before building. Keep it to facts and readings. Use plain words: the user must be able to spot a misreading in one pass.

```
## Overall space
- What kind of place it is, its footprint and its approximate size (from scale cues).
- Vertical layers, from lowest to highest, with heights.
- What encloses it: walls, cliffs, open sky. What lies beyond each edge of the frame.

## Camera
- Where the viewer stands: height above the ground, and position in the space.
- Where they look: the horizon row in the frame, and the pitch.
- The lens class: normal, slightly long or wide. Say how you read it (vanishing points, the convergence of verticals, how big near and far objects of the same type appear).

## Structure, element by element
For each signature element, then each secondary one:
- What it physically is, and how it is made (carved, applied, assembled, grown).
- What it stands on or connects to, and how it continues off-frame.
- Its key proportions and counts (bays, tiers, columns, arches).

## Light
- Sun direction (azimuth relative to the view, elevation), with the evidence.
- Key-to-fill impression, the colour of light versus shade, haze, and where bloom appears.

## Style
- Stylized or photographic. Where deviation from physics will live.

## Deviations
- AI-generated or impossible areas, each with the plausible version.
- Readings I'm unsure of, each phrased as a question with my recommended reading.
```

Phrase uncertain readings as questions the user can answer with one word ("The ring at the top: a hole cut in the ceiling, or an arch? I read it as a hole because the sky shows through it.").
